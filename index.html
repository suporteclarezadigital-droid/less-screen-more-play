Being present also means staying involved.

Awareness, not fear

Your child is growing up in a world they are still learning to understand.

The internet offers learning, entertainment and discovery. Without guidance, some challenges may be easy to miss—not because technology is always harmful, but because children are still learning how to navigate it.

01▤

Content that is not age-appropriate

Videos and posts a child may not yet have the maturity to understand.

02◎

Outside influences

Messages and behaviours that may shape how a child sees themselves and the world.

03◷

Finding it hard to switch off

It can be difficult to stop a game, video or favourite show.

04♡

Less room for other experiences

Fewer opportunities to play, explore, talk and try something new.

05⌂

Less time for family connection

It can be harder to find time for conversation and shared attention.

The challenge is not simply having a screen at home. It is making sure technology does not take the place of the guidance, presence and experiences children need as they grow.

What matters most

Your child needs more than less screen time. They need guidance as they grow.

No parent can control everything a child may come across online. But you can help them build healthier habits, talk about what they see and learn to make safer choices over time.

You can also create something no app can replace: moments of attention, play, conversation and family connection.

“This is not about keeping your child away from technology. It is about making sure technology does not take the place of your presence.”

Small steps that fit your family

Build healthier digital habits together.

Practical ideas for parents and caregivers who want to guide their children's digital habits and make more room for connection beyond screens.

✓ 01 / BOUNDARIES

Agree on clear limits

Set realistic screen-use boundaries with rules your family understands and can revisit together.

◷ 02 / ROUTINES

Make room for screen-free moments

Build routines that do not rely on videos or games to fill every quiet moment.

✳ 03 / PLAY

Find other ways to explore

Discover play and activities that suit your child's age, interests and everyday life.

“ 04 / CONVERSATION

Talk about what they see online

Make it easier to talk about content, outside influences and situations that deserve attention.

◎ 05 / GUIDANCE

Stay involved with curiosity

Take an active, age-appropriate interest in how your child uses technology.

♡ 06 / CONNECTION

Protect time to be together

Create family moments that strengthen your connection, both on and away from screens.

You do not have to change everything at once. Start with one small step that feels right at home.

Before you begin

Frequently asked questions.

A balanced approach starts with clear information and realistic expectations.

What ages is this guide designed for?

It was created for parents and caregivers of children aged 3–10.

Is the goal to stop my child using the internet?

No. The goal is to help families build more balanced habits and encourage age-appropriate, guided use of technology.

Does the guide protect children from every online risk?

No. It does not replace parental supervision or guarantee protection from every online risk.

Do I need to remove screens completely?

No. The aim is to find a balance between technology, play, everyday activities and family interaction.
